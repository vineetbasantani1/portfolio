Where shop data goes out of spec

Most of this is invisible from the owner's side. It shows up as missed searches, split stock counts and customers who can't tell two items apart.

  • Titles copied straight from a distributor feed

    Strings like "WR;OE;SGL;BK;3-3/4" mean something to a parts clerk and nothing to a customer or a search engine.

  • One category for every product

    Gloves, blades, hoses and sockets all filed under the same heading makes browsing and filtering useless.

  • Thin or missing descriptions

    A page with only a model number gives buyers nothing to compare and gives search engines nothing to match.

  • Duplicate records and mixed spellings

    "Ironvale", "IRONVALE" and "Ironvale Tools" count as three brands in a filter, and two entries for one part split its sales history.

  • Unit formats that disagree

    18V, 18 V and 18 volt; 1/2", 1/2 in and 12.7 mm. Reports and filters treat each as something different.

  • Stock counts that don't match the shelf

    The store says in stock, the bin says empty. That is a quiet source of refunds and lost trust.
