The problem

The mess hides in plain sight

Most shop owners never see these — they only show up if someone actually goes looking.

✓
Duplicate listings, different prices
The same product gets listed twice over time — customers see two prices and don't know which is real.
✓
Inconsistent titles across similar products
One naming style today, another six months ago — makes the shop harder to browse and hurts search visibility.
✓
"In stock" items that are actually sold out
A mismatch between what the store says is available and what's really there — a quiet source of lost sales.
✓
Custom orders tracked only in old messages
Measurements, color requests, and repeat customers get buried in DMs with no way to search back through them.
✓
Typos, broken links, leftover placeholder text
Small things — but they're the first impression a new customer gets of the business.
